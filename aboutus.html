About Us

Who We Are

Angela's Hope Foundation is a non-profit organisation committed to creating positive change in communities through sustainable development.

What We Do

We work directly with communities to identify needs and implement sustainable solutions that empower individuals and families.

Our Story

Angela's Hope Foundation was established with a vision to create a world where every child has the opportunity to thrive. Our journey began with a simple yet powerful idea: that every child deserves a future filled with hope, dignity, and endless possibilities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide education, food support, and community development programmes that empower individuals and families to break the cycle of poverty.

Our Vision

A world where every child has the opportunity to thrive and reach their full potential.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Empowerment, Sustainability, and Collaboration.
