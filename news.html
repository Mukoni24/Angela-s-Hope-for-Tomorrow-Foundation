News & Events

    Volunteer Appreciation Event

    Date: 12 March 2026

    Angela’s Hope Foundation hosted a special event to express gratitude and appreciation for the dedicated volunteers
    who contribute their time and efforts to support the foundation's initiatives.
    The event included recognition awards, speeches from community leaders, and opportunities for volunteers to share their experiences.
    Residents were encouraged to join the foundation as volunteers and make a positive impact in their community.

    Donation Drive

    Date: 5 May 2026

    Angela’s Hope Foundation organized a donation drive to collect essential items for families in need .
    The drive focused on gathering clothing, non-perishable food items, and school supplies.
    Residents were encouraged to contribute and support those in need, fostering a sense of community and solidarity.

    Community Clean-up Campaign

    Date: 10 June 2026

    Residents and volunteers combined their efforts to clean up common areas.
    This campaign encouraged people to be more responsible about their surroundings
    and created a cleaner environment for families in the area.

    Community Food Drive

    Date: 15 July 2026

    Our volunteers united to ensure that food packages were distributed to people who lacked food security.
    The project ensured that people were offered necessary foods while encouraging interaction among the community members.

    Upcoming Events

      Youth Skills Development Workshop

      Date: 5 August 2026

      We are hosting a skills development workshop to empower young people with essential skills for their future careers.

      Back-to-School Drive

      Date: 1 September 2026

      We are organizing a back-to-school drive to provide school supplies to children in need.
      Residents are encouraged to donate backpacks, stationery, and other educational materials.
      This initiative aims to ensure that every child has the necessary tools for a successful academic year.

      Community Health & Wellness Day

      Date: 15 October 2026

      We are hosting a community health and wellness day to promote healthy living and well-being.
      The event will feature health screenings, fitness activities, and educational workshops on nutrition and mental health.
      Residents are encouraged to participate and learn valuable information to improve their overall health.

      Family & Community Fun Day

      Date: 5 November 2026

      We are organizing a family and community fun day to bring residents together for a day of entertainment, games, and community bonding.
      The event will include activities for all ages, food stalls, and live performances.
      Residents are encouraged to attend and enjoy a day of fun while strengthening community ties.
